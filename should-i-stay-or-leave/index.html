For the messy middle

A structured pause before a serious relationship choice

This is for the moment when "just trust your gut" is not enough. Maybe you love them and still feel exhausted. Maybe nothing is obviously wrong, but something is not settling. Maybe leaving feels dramatic, and staying feels like abandoning yourself.

  1. Name the actual decision

    Turn the fog into clear options: stay, leave, pause, repair, renegotiate, or gather more information.

  2. Check values and constraints

    Look at respect, safety, love, health, support, timing, money, and the cost of inaction.

  3. Leave with a report

    Get a private summary of tradeoffs, uncertainty, signals, and next steps you can revisit.

It will not tell you what to do.

Relationship decisions deserve more than a score or a generic answer. The tool is neutral: it helps you see the shape of the decision so your next conversation or next step is less reactive.
