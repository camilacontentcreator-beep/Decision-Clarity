For decision fatigue, not job boards

When burnout is doing the deciding for you

Burnout has a way of making every option feel like the only option. Decision Clarity gives the question a structure so exhaustion is not the only thing steering.

  • You're not sure if it's the job or just this season.

    Separate what's a bad week from what's a bad fit before you make a move you can't undo.

  • You keep imagining quitting but can't picture what's next.

    Turn the vague urge to leave into real options: change, negotiate, search, or stay with conditions.

  • You're scared you'll regret staying, or regret leaving.

    Weigh the cost of the job against the cost of walking away with nothing lined up.

What the flow covers

Decision framing, comparing your real options, checking values like stability, growth, health, and money, plus a private summary you can revisit before you act.

What it avoids

It does not tell you to quit or to stay, diagnose your workplace, or pretend a career choice is just a paycheck comparison. It gives you a clearer conversation with yourself before the next big move.

Before you send the email

Bring the exhaustion. Leave with a plan, not just a feeling.

The flow takes about 10 to 15 minutes and stays private in your browser.
