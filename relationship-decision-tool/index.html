Use cases

Built for decisions where feelings and facts both matter

A relationship decision is rarely a clean spreadsheet problem. The point is not to erase emotion. The point is to understand what your emotions may be signaling, what the facts actually support, and what each path would ask of you.

  • You are deciding whether to stay, leave, or pause.

    Compare the default path with real alternatives instead of looping through the same question at 2 a.m.

  • You need to prepare for a difficult conversation.

    Clarify what matters before you ask for repair, boundaries, space, or a concrete change.

  • You are not ready to decide yet.

    Identify what information, support, or time would make the decision more responsible.

What the flow covers

The short flow helps you name the relationship question, consider the timing, notice what is influencing you, and choose a practical next step.

What it avoids

It does not diagnose your relationship, replace therapy, or pretend a serious choice can be reduced to one number. It gives you a clearer conversation with yourself.
